What Foundora is

A guided path from idea to launch

Foundora is a proposed online business incubation platform for first-time entrepreneurs in the UAE. It helps people turn business concepts into structured startup strategies, so they can tackle common problems like limited business knowledge, financial planning, confidence and access to professional advice.

How it works

Instead of another generic chatbot, Foundora offers a guided entrepreneurship experience. You enter your concept, experience, budget, location and goals. The platform then suggests activities that fit your situation.

  • AI business assistant
  • Custom startup roadmaps
  • Educational articles and videos
  • Research templates and business plan generators
  • Financial planning resources and pitch preparation tools
  • Help from relevant specialists

Who it's for

UAE residents aged 18 to 35: university students, recent graduates and young professionals who want to open their first business.

PERSONA
A 22-year-old student at the University of Dubai planning a graphic design business, who needs advice on pricing, market research and business development.

The seven-step customer journey

  1. Develop the idea
  2. Identify customers
  3. Validate the market
  4. Build the business model
  5. Plan finances
  6. Prepare to launch
  7. Plan for growth
Unique selling proposition

Why Foundora

AI-assisted business advice, startup roadmaps, practical education and customer validation, combined in one beginner-oriented platform. The innovation is not AI on its own. It is AI joined with systematic entrepreneurship processes, UAE-based resources and evidence-based decisions.

Personalised guidance

Information is structured around each user's business concept, resources, experience and stage, instead of leaving them to interpret general content alone.

UAE business context

Local examples, business planning and links to sources of help. Initial advice on licensing, markets and business formation, with referrals to official authorities or qualified professionals where needed.

Structured roadmaps

Business development broken into manageable tasks, so beginners see how customers, demand, financial estimates and a launch plan connect.

Customer validation tools

Templates, survey guidelines, testing plans and evidence tracking help users check whether they meet real customer needs before investing heavily.

Specialist support

When users need more advanced help, they can consult experts, combining AI guidance with professional skills.

Evidence over assumptions

"Customers will buy this" is an assumption. Interview responses, pilot participation and actual purchases are evidence. Foundora keeps the two apart.

Common problems Foundora solves

  • Creating and testing business concepts
  • Finding market opportunities and target customers
  • Preparing startup budgets and business plans
  • Accessing professional advice and helpful materials
  • Following a structured process towards launching a business

Data protection

Foundora will follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021): securing personal data, collecting only what is needed and using proper security protocols. Before expanding, Foundora will test willingness to pay, subscription rates and institutional relationships.

Prototype & technology

How the platform is built

The prototype is an HTML, CSS and JavaScript interface with three key screens: onboarding, the startup roadmap dashboard and the AI assistant. You can try versions of all three at the top of this page.

Planned architecture

  • Front end: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and a modern web framework
  • Back end: a secure application server for accounts and business data
  • Database: user profiles, project information and progress
  • AI: a commercial AI model delivered through an API
  • Security: authentication, access controls, encryption, data-retention policies
  • Analytics: simple usage tracking

Not in the prototype yet

A live AI integration, payment system, specialist booking system and production database. These will be designed and tested before commercial rollout. The assistant on this page uses simulated responses.

Templates planned

  • Business plan and proposal templates
  • Market research templates
  • Financial worksheets
  • Investor pitch preparation

Coming next

  • Arabic language support
  • A more detailed financial calculator
  • Downloadable business plans
  • Better AI responses

Usability testing will check whether first-time founders can finish onboarding, understand their roadmap and know what to do next without much help. No customer testing results have been reported yet.
